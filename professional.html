Professional Journey

My career spans over seven years across industry, research, and academia — moving from cybersecurity operations at global financial institutions to graduate research at the intersection of hardware security and formal verification. Each role has deepened my conviction that security must be built from the ground up, starting at the hardware level.

Summer Research Fellow

US Department of Agriculture (USDA) & North Dakota State University
May – Aug 2025

As part of a USDA-funded project investigating factors contributing to bee population decline, I designed and implemented a battery monitoring system for remote field sensor networks. Using a Raspberry Pi Pico W and MicroPython, I integrated an INA219 voltage/current sensor, thermistor, and DS3231 RTC to track power consumption at 5-minute intervals. The system connected to Adafruit IO via MQTT to stream live telemetry data and generate real-time voltage decay graphs. The work demanded a focus on reliability and low-power operation for deployment in solar-powered, remote environments — a challenge that sharpened my skills in embedded systems and IoT design.

Cybersecurity Engineer & Team Lead

Barclays UK, Liverpool, United Kingdom
2022 – 2025

At Barclays, I led a team responsible for planning, implementing, and monitoring security measures across the organization's data, systems, and networks. A highlight of my tenure was leading the Advanced Persistent Threat (APT) Detection team in 2023, successfully identifying and mitigating a sophisticated APT attack — an effort that earned me the Award of Excellence for Employee of the Month. Day to day, I conducted vulnerability assessments, responded to network security breaches, managed security controls, and actively participated in the change management process to keep defenses current against evolving threats. I also volunteered with Barclays' Teardrops Initiative, contributing to community service efforts for vulnerable individuals in Liverpool.

Security Incident Response Group Head & Red Team Lead

Amazon UK
2021 – 2022

At Amazon UK, I served in two successive leadership roles. As Red Team Group Leader, I directed a team of ethical hackers to simulate real-world cyber-attacks, identifying vulnerabilities before adversaries could exploit them. I then moved into the Security Incident Response Group Head role, managing the team responsible for detecting, containing, and resolving security incidents across Amazon UK's infrastructure. This dual experience — both offensive and defensive — gave me a comprehensive perspective on how organisations can build resilient security postures. I was recognised with the Award of Excellence for Employee of the Month for teamwork and collaboration.

Cybersecurity Analyst & Red Team Leader

Zenith Bank Plc, Lagos, Nigeria
2018 – 2022

My industry career began at Zenith Bank, where I conducted regular vulnerability assessments and penetration testing, monitored network traffic using intrusion detection systems, and investigated security incidents through root cause analysis. I also led red team operations from 2019 to 2020, overseeing simulated attacks to stress-test the bank's defences and drive meaningful security improvements. This role grounded me in the realities of protecting critical financial infrastructure and motivated me to pursue deeper knowledge in hardware-level security.
