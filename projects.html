🔒 Hardware Security & Formal Verification

PCHB Trojan Detector

Research

A formal verification framework that detects stealthy Hardware Trojans in asynchronous Pre-Charge Half Buffer (PCHB) circuits by leveraging SMT-based property checking at the Register-Transfer Level. Achieved 100% detection accuracy across 28 RSA benchmark circuits.
