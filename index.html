Home

Welcome to my personal page. I am a doctoral researcher and engineer working at the intersection of hardware security, formal verification, cybersecurity, and computer architecture. At North Dakota State University, I explore how emerging threats can be detected and neutralized at the hardware level, before they ever reach software. I also bring years of hands-on industry experience in cybersecurity. This site showcases my research, professional journey, and the work I am most passionate about.

Bio

Growing up in Nigeria shaped my resilience, and it is what I have built since that defines my work today. I hold a B.Eng. in Electrical Engineering from Nnamdi Azikiwe University, Nigeria, and went on to spend years leading cybersecurity operations at two of the most demanding institutions in the UK — Amazon and Barclays UK, where I led red team operations, incident response, and security architecture at scale.

That industry experience brought me back to academia with sharper questions. I am now a Graduate Research and Teaching Assistant at North Dakota State University, where my work sits at the boundary of hardware security and formal verification. Outside the lab, I teach Digital Logic Design and Introduction to Computing, mentor students, and find that explaining hard ideas clearly is one of the most useful skills I have developed.

I am driven by the belief that the most important security problems are not yet solved, and that solving them requires people who have stood on both sides of the wall, in industry and in research.
